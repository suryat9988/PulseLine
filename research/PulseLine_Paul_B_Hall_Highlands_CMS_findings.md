## What engineering can use

The JSON preserves original financial, balance-sheet, staffing, payer and utilization columns. Each report includes CCN, CMS report ID, actual fiscal dates, source link through source_id, access date, scope limitations and explicit nulls. Map it through an adapter; it is not assumed compatible with the current application schema.

Paul B. Hall and Paintsville ARH appear under CCN 180078. Highlands Regional appears under CCN 180005. Name matching alone is unnecessary for these extracted rows. Current branding and transaction-effective dates remain separate verification tasks.

## Critical integration details

- The 2022 download contains three Paintsville/Paul B. Hall periods: October–November 2021, December 2021–June 2022, and July 2022–June 2023. Preserve each record. They are not duplicate annual totals.
- Highlands also may have nonstandard periods; use period_days and fiscal dates rather than assuming every record covers one year.
- Net patient revenue and operating expense are reported values; no validated operating margin has been calculated here. Financial scope may include hospital reporting components.
- All first-public-availability dates remain unknown. A report ending before an event is not automatically eligible for a historical prediction test. These are revised releases downloaded in 2026.
- No patient information or personal clinician histories were collected.
- This addition supplies data to research/. It does not change the dashboard, scoring engine, or source adapter. A developer must import these records before the pending-data message can be removed honestly.
